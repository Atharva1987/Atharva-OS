About Me

I’m Atharva, a Class 12 student and JEE aspirant from India, currently in one of the most intense phases of my academic journey.

I’m deeply interested in technology, AI, software, entrepreneurship, and building things. Long term, I want to create meaningful products and develop the skills to turn ideas into reality. But right now, my primary focus is learning how to work with discipline, consistency, and depth.

I’m fascinated by the intersection of productivity and technology, which led me to build Elysium — my personal productivity system. Elysium is designed around managing focus, habits, goals, missions, study progress, and personal growth. It has evolved into more than just a tracker; it’s an experiment in building a system that helps me actually execute.

I care more about capability than validation. I’m trying to become someone who can sit down, do difficult work, solve hard problems, learn from failure, and keep going without constantly needing external motivation or comparison.

This phase of my life is ultimately about building the foundation — academically, technically, and personally — for everything I want to do later.

Build capability. Stay curious. Execute. Repeat.
